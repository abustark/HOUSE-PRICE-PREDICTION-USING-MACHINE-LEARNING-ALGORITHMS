    Zero-Contamination Protocol

    The 70/30 train/test split strictly preceded any preprocessing. All learned transformations (BHK medians, locality frequencies, StandardScalers) were fitted exclusively on the training slice and applied downstream.

    Location GroupKFold Stress Test

    Evaluated generalization to completely unseen locations using GroupKFold. Random Forest achieved a 0.719 Group-CV R², quantifying the expected realistic penalty when encountering novel localities.
